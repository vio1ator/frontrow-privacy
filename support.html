Support & FAQ

Every seat is front row. Here are quick answers on recording modes, offline voice isolation, mixing, and exporting.

How should I position my iPhone for Table vs. Stage mode?

Table mode (default) uses your iPhone’s omnidirectional bottom microphone—lay your phone flat on the table to capture voices evenly in 360°. Stage mode switches to the directional back microphone—hold or prop your iPhone with the rear cameras facing the speaker across the room.

Does Frontrow upload my audio to the cloud?

No. Recording, neural voice isolation, mixing, and exporting all happen 100% locally on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I lock my iPhone while Frontrow isolates a recording?

Yes. Once you stop recording, voice isolation starts automatically and continues in the background with a Live Activity progress bar on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Keeping Frontrow open in the foreground uses your iPhone’s GPU for maximum speed; locking your phone switches seamlessly to optimized background CPU processing.

How do the Room ↔ Front Row slider and Voice Boost work?

Frontrow keeps your original 48 kHz recording untouched and separates speech into a phase-locked stem. Slide smoothly between Room (0%), Natural (50%), and Front Row (100%) to keep natural room ambience or isolate pure speech, tap Mix / Raw to A/B compare instantly, and toggle Voice Boost to lift quiet far-field speakers cleanly without clipping.

Can I clean existing Voice Memos or export lossless files?

Yes. Tap the top-right Import icon on the Library screen to bring in any audio file from Files or Voice Memos. Inside any session, tap the top-right Share icon to export your Current Mix, Isolated Voice, or Original Raw recording as 48 kHz M4A or 24-bit WAV.
